# Content Plan

## Current decisions and preserved ideas (September 2026)

The original outline below is retained as planning history, not a requirement to
restore every section. Current page responsibilities:

- Home: headshot/candid slots, a short personal introduction, and themed interests.
  No project previews, academic showcase, or full photo gallery.
- About: longer personal story, interests, and future candid/memory material.
- Photography: personal collections, animal/plant categories, scenery, people,
  miscellaneous photographs, collage layouts, and a separate live iNaturalist feed.
- Projects: selected work, chronological archive, detail pages, and future ideas.
  Existing role, tools, status, notes, and project links remain on detail pages.
- Activities: dated leadership, research roles, competitions, teaching, and service,
  with expandable artifact placeholders.
- Contact: email and public profiles. A resume was in the original plan but still
  needs a user-approved PDF before adding a download link.
- Design browser: separate effect-selection workspace with saved picks and notes.

Preserve for later, without adding empty top-level pages now:

- Writing / field notes: original ROADMAP.md includes a blog/writing system.
- Project process: sketches, iterations, screenshots, demonstrations, and lessons.
- Supplementary portfolio: concise curated work, contribution/credit, evidence,
  methods and limitations for research, and optional downloadable material.
- Personal scrapbook: real memories, tickets, drawings, or keepsakes from the user.
- Selected Drive-folder photo import: planned only; no Drive connection exists.
- Optional Easter egg and richer image transitions: choose via the design browser.

Missing user input: introduction, actual hobbies, headshot/candid photos, personal
photo collections, project artifacts, future project ideas, and optional resume.
